About the Program

Sobha Innovation Scout – Poland & CEE is an innovation scouting initiative focused on identifying high-potential technology companies from Central and Eastern Europe with solutions that can shape the future of real estate, construction, and the built environment.

Selected startups will participate in a structured evaluation process, culminating in an online Pitch Day where they will present their solutions to Sobha's innovation and business leaders.

The most promising companies will be invited to Dubai for an exclusive one-week immersion programme, providing the opportunity to engage directly with Sobha's teams, explore pilot projects, assess commercial applications, and develop strategic collaborations with one of the region's leading real estate developers.

Exceptional participants may also be considered for future investment opportunities and long-term strategic partnerships.
