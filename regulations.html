Before You Apply

We encourage all applicants to review the Programme Regulations before submitting an application. The document outlines the eligibility criteria, evaluation process, confidentiality, intellectual property, personal data, and other terms governing the Programme.

The complete Regulations can be downloaded using the button above.

1. General Provisions

The Programme is designed to identify, evaluate, and select startups, scale-ups, and established companies from Poland and across Central and Eastern Europe whose solutions align with Sobha's business priorities.

The Programme welcomes companies developing innovative solutions across sectors including real estate, construction, smart buildings, furniture manufacturing, retail, logistics, sustainability, artificial intelligence, the Internet of Things, and other areas relevant to Sobha's business.

The Programme is intended to create opportunities for future collaboration between selected Applying Companies and Sobha, including pilot projects, commercial engagements, strategic partnerships, and potential investment opportunities. Participation in the Programme does not constitute an offer or guarantee of future cooperation.

2. Eligibility

The Programme is open to Applying Companies legally registered in Poland or another country within Central and Eastern Europe.

Each Applying Company must operate as a registered business. The individual submitting the Application must be authorised to represent the Applying Company or hold the necessary authority to participate in the Programme on its behalf.

The Organiser or Sobha may request documentation confirming the Applying Company's registration, the Participant's authority to act on its behalf, ownership or rights relating to the submitted solution or technology, and any additional information required for the evaluation process.

All individuals participating on behalf of an Applying Company are required to review and comply with these Rules.

3. Program Process

The Programme is expected to follow these stages:

  • Application Submission
  • Initial Review
  • Recommendation to Sobha
  • Sobha Evaluation
  • Pitch Day Selection
  • Pitch Day
  • Winner Selection
  • Working Visit

Up to ten Applying Companies may be invited to participate in Pitch Day.

Programme timelines and key dates will be published on the Website or communicated electronically.

Prior to participating in Pitch Day or the Working Visit, shortlisted companies may be required to execute additional agreements relating to confidentiality, information sharing, programme logistics, success fees, or future collaboration.

The Sobha Committee will select at least one Winner and reserves the right to recognise multiple Winners.

4. How to Apply

Applications must be submitted by completing and submitting the online application form available on the Website.

Participants are responsible for ensuring that all information provided is accurate, complete, and up to date.

Each Applying Company may submit one Application only.

5. Evaluation Process

Applications will be assessed against a range of criteria, including strategic relevance, innovation, solution maturity, implementation potential, scalability, team capability, commercial viability, readiness for international collaboration, and overall risk profile.

The initial evaluation will be conducted by the Recruitment Committee appointed by Innteo.

Pitch Day evaluations and the final selection of Winners will be conducted by the Sobha Committee.

6. Dubai Working Visit

Sobha will invite at least one Winner to participate in a one-week Working Visit in Dubai.

Unless otherwise agreed in writing, each invited Winner will be represented by a minimum of two participants.

Sobha will cover approved travel expenses, including round-trip airfare and accommodation.

Participants are responsible for all other expenses, including local transportation, meals, visas, insurance, daily allowances, and any costs not expressly covered by Sobha.

7. Program Costs & Success Fee

Participation in the Programme is free of charge.

Each Applying Company is responsible for its own participation costs, with the exception of approved Working Visit expenses covered by Sobha.

Companies selected to participate in Pitch Day will be required to enter into a separate agreement with Innteo regarding the applicable success fee.

8. Intellectual Property and Confidentiality

All intellectual property rights remain the property of the Applying Company or the respective rights holder.

By participating in the Programme, the Applying Company grants Innteo and Sobha a limited, non-exclusive right to use submitted materials solely for Programme-related evaluation and administration.

The Applying Company confirms that it holds all necessary rights and permissions relating to the submitted materials.

The names, logos, trademarks, and other identifying marks of Innteo and Sobha may not be used without prior written consent.

9. Personal Data

Personal data will be processed for the administration of the Programme, evaluation of Applications, participant communications, and the exploration of potential collaboration opportunities.

Details regarding data controllers, legal bases, recipients, retention periods, and participant rights will be outlined in a separate Privacy Notice.

10. Participant Responsibilities

Participants and Applying Companies are expected to comply with these Rules, provide accurate information, and adhere to all applicable legal and confidentiality requirements.

Failure to comply with these Rules, submission of false information, non-cooperation, legal violations, or other specified misconduct may result in exclusion from the Programme.

11. Questions & Concerns

Questions, concerns, or complaints relating to the Programme may be submitted to:

Email: projekty@innteo.com

Complaints should include the company name, contact details, a description of the issue, the alleged irregularity, and the requested resolution.

12. Additional Information

These Rules are available on the Programme Website.

Programme-related communications may be sent electronically using the email address provided in the Application.

Where necessary, Innteo may issue additional organisational guidance, provided it does not conflict with these Rules.

Any dispute arising in connection with the Programme should first be resolved through amicable discussions.
