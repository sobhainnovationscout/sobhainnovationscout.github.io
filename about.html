About the Program

Learn more about Sobha Realty and Innteo, the companies behind this initiative, and the mission of the Program.

Sobha Realty

At Sobha, we believe exceptional developments are built through exceptional attention to detail. From concept to completion, we bring together craftsmanship, thoughtful design, and uncompromising quality to create places that stand the test of time.

Our success is driven by the expertise of our people. Engineers, architects, designers, and specialists collaborate with a shared commitment to excellence, delivering innovative solutions that shape the future of the built environment.

Innovation is integral to our approach. By combining deep industry expertise with emerging technologies and forward-thinking ideas, we continue to redefine what's possible across real estate, construction, and design.

Every detail reflects our pursuit of excellence. Every project represents our commitment to creating enduring value for generations to come.

Our Mission

The Sobha Innovation Scout – CEE programme exists to discover technologies that can shape the future of real estate, construction, and the built environment.

We bring together innovative startups and Sobha's experts to explore practical solutions, encourage collaboration, and unlock opportunities for pilot projects, strategic partnerships, and long-term innovation.

By combining entrepreneurial thinking with industry expertise, we aim to accelerate ideas that create meaningful and lasting impact.

Our Values

Craftsmanship

We value expertise, precision, and attention to detail, recognising that exceptional outcomes are achieved through skill, dedication, and a commitment to excellence.

Thoughtful Design

We seek ideas that combine creativity with purpose, delivering practical solutions that address real challenges and create lasting value.

Signature Quality

We uphold the highest standards across everything we do, championing quality, integrity, and solutions designed for long-term impact.

Program Partner

The program is organized in cooperation with Innteo, an innovation and technology partner supporting the recruitment process, communication and collaboration with participating companies.

Innteo contributes its experience in working with startups, innovation projects and international business development, ensuring an efficient and transparent application process.
